## Frequently asked questions

### Can I buy a same-day ticket?

Some productions offer same-day tickets, but availability differs by show. Check the official website, the theater box office, or the company’s social media before leaving.

### How much should I budget?

For the performances listed here, a realistic budget is ¥2,000–¥5,000 for many small-theater productions, ¥6,000–¥9,000 for musicals and established productions, and up to ¥13,000 for the highest-priced seats.

### Do I need Japanese to enjoy these performances?

Language access varies. Check the official production page for English information, captions, surtitles, or visual-performance details. Even when a show is primarily in Japanese, venue staff may be able to help with basic access questions.

### Where can I find Japanese theater scripts?

Gikyoku Tosyokan, the Theater Script Library, lets readers search Japanese plays by cast size, runtime, genre, and other practical criteria. It is useful for theater companies, drama clubs, translators, and anyone who wants to explore the scripts behind Japanese stage productions.
